How to read these estimates

Exploring — little evidence or a low estimate. Developing — at least two attempts, estimate ≥ 40%. Proficient — four or more attempts, ≥ 70%. Mastered — six or more attempts, ≥ 85%, low uncertainty and at least one successful delayed recall.

Confidence reflects how much evidence there is. Estimates describe performance on SkillForge challenges — useful for choosing practice, not a certification.
