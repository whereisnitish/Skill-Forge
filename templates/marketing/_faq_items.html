How is SkillForge different from a course or a question bank?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Courses follow a fixed order and question banks are random. SkillForge keeps a model of what you know, and every answer you give changes what it recommends next: weak areas, reviews you're due for, prerequisites you're missing and the priorities of your goal.
How are my answers graded?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Deterministically. Multiple-choice and short answers are checked against answer keys. Written explanations and code reviews are scored against a published rubric of key ideas. Coding challenges run against test cases in an isolated sandbox. The AI mentor can explain a result but never changes it.
What does the mastery percentage mean?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
It's an estimate of how likely you are to succeed on a typical challenge for that skill, based on your evidence so far. It always comes with a confidence level. Harder challenges count for more, hints count for less, and estimates fade gradually if you stop practising. It is a learning aid, not a certification.
How much time do I need?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
You choose: 10, 15, 20, 30 or 45 minutes a day. The daily plan fits challenges to that budget, and you can always practise more.
Do I need the AI mentor?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
No. Everything works without it. When the AI mentor isn't available you'll see curated guidance instead, clearly labelled as such.

Is my code executed safely?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Code runs only in a separate sandbox service with no network access, strict time, memory and process limits, and no access to application secrets. If the sandbox isn't available, coding challenges say so — they are never marked as passed without running.
Can I change my goal later?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Yes. You can create several goals, pause or archive them, change your daily time and choose skills to prioritise. Changes affect future recommendations; your past attempts are kept exactly as they were.
What happens to my data if I delete my account?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Deleting your account from Settings permanently removes your profile, goals, attempts, mastery history and notifications. Paid subscriptions are cancelled with the payment provider first.
Does SkillForge guarantee a job or interview result?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
No. SkillForge helps you practise deliberately and see your progress honestly. Outcomes depend on many things we don't control, and we won't pretend otherwise.
Can I cancel a paid plan?{% icon "plus" "size-5" %}
Yes, at any time from the billing portal. You keep paid features until the end of the period you've paid for.
