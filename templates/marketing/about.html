What we believe

Skills grow through deliberate practice: working at the edge of your ability, getting specific feedback, and coming back before you forget. That loop is simple to describe and hard to run on your own. SkillForge runs it for you.

We think learning tools should be honest. That means showing how confident an estimate is, explaining why a challenge was chosen, and never pretending an answer was evaluated when it wasn't.

Evidence over vibes

Progress is measured from evaluated attempts, never from time spent or streaks alone.

Explain every decision

Recommendations carry a stored reason you can read.

Deterministic where it matters

Scores come from tests and rubrics. AI explains; it doesn't grade.

Your data is yours

Private by default, deletable at any time from Settings.

Where we are

SkillForge is an early-stage product. The curriculum focuses on software engineering today, with communication skills as a first step beyond it. We'd love to hear what would make it more useful to you.
