Mastery you can trust — including its uncertainty.

Estimates are built from evidence, weighted by difficulty and independence. Hints and revealed solutions count for less. Successful recall after a gap counts for more. Nothing is labelled “Mastered” without repeated evidence and a successful delayed review.
